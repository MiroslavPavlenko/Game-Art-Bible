## 3. Political / Societal Map

Provide **1 map** showing who lives where, layered on top of your terrain map.
![[Berlin’s Whimsical Occupation Map.png]]
The world is divided between multiple factions fighting for control of Berlin. The **Potatoes (CCCR)** control the northwestern territories, where they build farms, settlements, and military bases. The **Worms (Nazis)** control the eastern territories, where they destroy cities and build underground tunnels. The British and American factions control the western and southern parts of the city. The map was created by a potato military cartographer working for the CCCR to document enemy territories and important locations. Since he works for the potatoes, the map mostly shows their perspective of the war. Some areas in the east are left unexplored because nobody knows what is happening there, and many potato scouts who traveled there never returned.

## 4. Timeline

- April 16 — The invasion begins
    
    The CCCR potato army starts its final attack on Berlin. Worm forces prepare to defend the city.
    
- April 25 — Berlin is surrounded
    
    The potato army surrounds the city. Most gardens are destroyed during the fighting, leaving only a few safe locations.
    
- April 29 — The game begins
    
    The player is a normal potato living in a Frenchman's garden. With the war getting closer, the player must escape and survive.
    
- April 30 — The German leader dies
    
    The leader of the Worm regime takes his own life. The player is unaware of what happened because of the chaos around the city.
    
- May 2 — Berlin falls
    
    The defending Worm forces surrender. Depending on the player's actions, different characters may survive or die.
    
- May 8 — The war ends in Europe
    
    Germany's defeat becomes official. The future of the remaining potato and insect settlements is unknown.
    
The timeline is recorded by a potato historian who works for the CCCR after the war. He collects information from soldiers, civilians, and surviving documents to explain what happened during the final days of Berlin. Since he works for the CCCR, most of the information focuses on the potato army and their victories, while the stories of normal people and the Worms are mostly left out.

Some parts of the timeline are incomplete because many documents were destroyed during the fighting. One of the biggest mysteries is what happened to the last surviving garden in Berlin. The historian doesn't know what happened to the potatoes living there. The player will experience this part of history through the roguelike gameplay, where each run can show different events and characters. The player's actions won't change the outcome of the war, but they can change what happens to the people they meet.

## 5. Art Bible Crosscheck

One of the main locations in my game will be the last surviving garden in Berlin. Like I mentioned in my Game Art Bible, I'm planning to use an art style inspired by Hades with hand-painted textures, pre-drawn shadows, and lighting, while keeping the simplicity and readability of The Binding of Isaac. Since the player is a potato, the environment will feel microscopic, where normal plants, rocks, and other objects will look like massive structures. I will use a limited but colorful palette to make the game feel less serious, even though it's taking place during the final days of World War II. The background will show destroyed buildings and signs of war, while the garden itself will still feel alive. This contrast will help tell the story through the environment without directly explaining everything to the player.
