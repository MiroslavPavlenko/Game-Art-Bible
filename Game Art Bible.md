# Art Style  
For my game I was thinking making game inspired by art style and mechanics from game called Hades but at the same time have vibe of The Binding of Isaac with simplicity of levels and clear readability of the characters and ability even in chaos of the game. It will be stylysed characters and levels with pre drawn shadows and light with hand painted vibe. It will be set in the garden in 1942 time period where player as a potato has to escape garden of Polish farmer where he has to defeat enemy's and get stronger over time.

# Technical Guidelines
For character design and sculpting I will use Z-Brush, for basic level block out and other models I will use Blender/Maya, I will use maya for animations and motion capture clean up, for textures i will use Adobe substance painter 3d. All of those tools have capability with unity and can export and bundle staff that unity can accept.
